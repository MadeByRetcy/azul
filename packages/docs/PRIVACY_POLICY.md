# Collected data and information
Azul uses OAuth 2.0 (With Github & Msharfin as provided), as such it collects/stores the following types of personal information from you:
- E-mail address
- OAuth Provider Data:
    - Full name & username
    - Avatar
This data is removed/deleted when you delete your account and cancel your Itri subscription.

All personal data is encrypted and protected from any unauthorized non Made by Retcy entity, contributors/maintainers as such don't have access; Please note that we use essential cookies for session management and authentication to keep you logged in.

**N.B:** We may share your personal information with authorities in case of a warrant/legal request.
# User rights
Azul users have the same rights as Moroccan law no. 09-08 provides, to exercise any of these rights, please contact us via E-mail [support@msharf.in](mailto:support@msharf.in).
# Data Security
We try our best to take steps to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These steps include:

- Using industry-standard security measures, such as encryption.
- Restricting access to your personal information to authorized employees.

Please note that these security measures won’t necessarily mean immunity from unauthorized access, however, we’re committed to protecting your data and take all necessary measures to do so.
